Good to Know

What affects your project's price?

Every project is unique. Here are the factors that influence the final quote.

Number of Pages

More pages means more design, content, and development work.

Integrations

Payment gateways, APIs, CRM connections, and third-party services.

Content Management

Adding an admin panel or CMS adds backend complexity.

User Accounts

Authentication, roles, profiles, and permission systems.

Payments

Checkout flows, subscription billing, and invoicing systems.

Dashboards & Analytics

Data visualisation, reporting, and real-time metrics.

Design Complexity

Custom animations, micro-interactions, and complex layouts.

Timeline
