About 4 Chis Construction & Projects cc

Proven Civil & Building Contractors Based in Haddon, Gauteng

Founded and led by Mr. Kennedy Williams, 4 Chis Construction & Projects cc has established itself as a premier construction and engineering company serving all government SETA sectors across South Africa.

From our headquarters in Haddon, Gauteng, SA (located south of Johannesburg), we deliver disciplined excellence in every project. Our track record spans landmark public works including the Chris Hani Baragwanath Hospital Medical Workshop, municipal infrastructure for Merafong City Municipality, and residential community developments exceeding 1,300 houses built.

We operate with uncompromising safety, technical rigor, and hands-on site oversight across healthcare, municipal, educational, commercial, and residential sectors.
