Deployment Methodology

Site protection & operational protocol

How KUIPFI structures, deploys, and governs security operations to ensure zero breakdown in compliance, vigilance, or response.

Stage 01

Site Threat & Compliance Audit

Baseline security survey, perimeter vulnerability scoring, access flow evaluation, and formulation of site-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

Stage 02

Vetted Guarding Deployment

Deployment of PSIRA-accredited officers (Grades A–C), tailored briefing on post orders, verified PPE/equipment issue, and prime contractor SLA sign-off.

Stage 03

24/7 Operations Oversight

Continuous supervisor spot-checks, Occurrence Book (OB) monitoring, offsite CCTV integration, and direct radio communication with our central control desk.

Stage 04

Incident Governance & Audit

Formal incident escalation reports, digital patrol logs, monthly vendor file reviews, and ongoing statutory reconciliation (NBCPSS, UIF, COID).

Statutory Due Diligence

Enterprise & subcontractor readiness register

A comprehensive document register for corporate procurement panels, main contractors, and Fidelity subcontractor onboarding packs. Confirming full statutory compliance across tax, labor, firearm accreditation, and private security regulations.

Company & tax records
  1. Completed credit check application form
  2. CSD registration report
  3. B-BBEE EME affidavit or QSE certificate
  4. Current CIPC disclosure with directors
  5. Latest original SARS tax PIN letter
  6. VAT 103 registration document
  7. Current COID and UIF records, including recent EMP201 proof
  8. Insurance schedule and certificates
  9. Banking details verification letter
Security-sector compliance
  1. Valid PSIRA certificate
  2. PSIRA letter of good standing with current directors
  3. National Bargaining Council (NBCPSS) registration and good-standing letter
  4. Affinity Health letter of good standing
  5. SANSEA registration proof
  6. Private Security Provident Fund monthly compliance letter
  7. ICASA proof where applicable
  8. Valid SAIDSA certificate
  9. Management team IDs and valid management PSIRA records
Directors & company profile
  1. Directors' SAPS 365 clearance certificates, not older than three months
  2. Company profile and physical branch addresses
  3. Municipal accounts for offices in the company name
  4. Office lease agreements and six months' proof of payment
  5. Share certificate, shareholding information and organogram
  6. Three years of annual financial statements and asset summary
  7. References not older than 12 months
  8. CVs of owners and directors
Firearms, vehicles & specialist services
  1. Company SAPS firearm accreditation
  2. Valid firearm licences and SAPS firearm list
  3. National Key Point registration proof, if applicable
  4. Proof of vehicles registered in the company name
  5. RC1 vehicle registration papers and vehicle list
  6. PAPA licence for dogs, if canine services are supplied
Operational employment records
  1. Latest guard payslips for Grades A, B and C
  2. Area 1 and Area 3 payslip samples, where required
  3. Current UIF evidence and employment compliance records
  4. Current provident-fund compliance evidence

This register is maintained as an active vendor readiness file for rapid submission to prime contractor onboarding portals, tender committees, and joint venture evaluations.
