  5. Latest original SARS tax PIN confirmation letter
  6. VAT 103 registration document
  7. Current COID and UIF records with recent EMP201 proof

Security Sector Regulatory Adherence
  1. Valid PSIRA company registration certificate
  2. PSIRA letter of good standing with active directors
  3. National Bargaining Council (NBCPSS) registration letter
  4. Affinity Health letter of good standing
  5. Private Security Provident Fund monthly compliance letter
  6. Valid SAIDSA certificate
  7. Management team PSIRA certificates and clearances
Directors, Facilities & Governance
  1. Directors' SAPS 365 clearance certificates (under 3 months)
  2. Company profile with physical branch locations
  3. Municipal accounts and office lease proof of payment

  5. Annual financial statements and asset register
  6. Client reference letters not older than 12 months
