The story behind the build

How we helped these businesses solve real problems with custom web solutions.

Construction & Infrastructure

Makhaswa Holdings

The Challenge

Makhaswa Holdings needed an online presence that reflected the scale and professionalism of their construction and infrastructure operations. They had no website and were losing credibility with potential clients and tender boards.

What We Built

  • Responsive multi-page corporate website
  • Project gallery showcasing completed builds
  • Custom quote request forms
  • SEO optimisation for local construction searches

The Result

A professional digital presence that strengthened Makhaswa's credibility with clients and tender authorities, leading to an increase in direct enquiries and project proposals.

Environmental Services

Amabongo Solutions

The Challenge

Amabongo Solutions, a glass recycling organisation, had an outdated website that wasn't generating leads. They needed a modern platform to attract recycling load sellers and industry partners.

What We Built

  • Complete website redesign with modern UI
  • Clear service breakdown and call-to-action flows
  • SEO strategy targeting recycling-related searches
  • Mobile-optimised contact and enquiry forms

The Result

Following the new platform launch, Amabongo saw a massive rise in calls, emails, and recycling load enquiries from both sellers and potential partners — a direct impact on their business pipeline.

Vehicle Administration

AJ Vehicle Registration

The Challenge

AJ Vehicle Registration offers a range of administrative services — registrations, licensing renewals, deregistration, and compliance documentation — but had no professional website to explain their services or capture leads.

What We Built

  • Clean, professional service website
  • Detailed service breakdowns with clear pricing
  • Contact forms tied to specific service enquiries
  • Fast-loading static site hosted on GitHub Pages

The Result

A professional online presence that clearly communicates AJ's service offerings, making it easy for customers to understand what's available and reach out with confidence.
