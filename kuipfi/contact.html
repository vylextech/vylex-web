Procurement & Engagement FAQs

Standard operating procedures for enterprise onboarding and consulting engagements.

What is the turnaround time for a formal proposal or risk assessment quote?
Our commercial advisory team reviews procurement submissions within 24 business hours. Preliminary proposals or risk assessment fee scopes are typically issued within 2 business days following initial site or document review.
How can our procurement committee request the complete Vendor Due Diligence Pack?
You can select "Vendor File / Due Diligence Pack" in the enquiry form above or email tenders@kuipfi.co.za directly with your RFQ details. Our statutory pack contains verified copies of PSIRA, NBCPSS, SAPS accreditation, B-BBEE, SARS PIN, and COID certificates.
Does KUIPFI support multi-province deployments across South Africa?
Yes. KUIPFI Strategic Risk Consultants deploys across commercial campuses, logistics networks, and industrial facilities nationwide, with established regional operational command hubs in Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, and the Western Cape.
